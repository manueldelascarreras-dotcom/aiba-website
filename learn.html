The part that actually matters

You get good at AI by building, not reading.

Pick one task you personally hate — a spreadsheet you keep redoing, an email you rewrite every time, research you repeat each week — and automate it with the tools above. Then post what you built in the club channel. Do that every week. That is the entire method.
