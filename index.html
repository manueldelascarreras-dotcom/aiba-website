Featured event
RT Richmond TaylorFounder, Bishop AI & PromptAnything.io
Hands-on workshop · UStart × AIBA

Build Your Own AI Operating System

  • Tue, September 8, 2026
  • 6:30 – 8:00 PM
  • Shalala Senate Room
  • Richmond Taylor · Founder, Bishop AI
RSVP Now
What you'll learn

Tap any topic for what it covers.

The patterns that make AI genuinely useful: giving context, showing an example, asking for the exact format you want, and iterating instead of settling for the first answer. You'll leave able to get a solid draft, analysis, or plan out of Claude or ChatGPT in a try or two.

Use tools like Cursor, v0, and no-code builders to turn an idea into a working web app, bot, or internal tool — no CS background required. We walk a small project end to end so you've actually shipped something.

Spot the repetitive tasks in your week — reformatting data, drafting the same emails, pulling research — and wire them into automations that run on their own. Small time savings that compound across a semester.

How AI is really used in finance (research, risk, modeling), marketing (segmentation, content, analytics), and operations (forecasting, support). Concrete use cases you can bring straight into an internship or class project.

Use AI to move faster on research without getting burned: checking sources, catching hallucinations, structuring what you find, and citing it properly. Built for case competitions, papers, and market research.

Just enough of the mechanics behind large language models — training, context windows, why they hallucinate, what they can't do — to use them well and talk about them credibly. No math required.
